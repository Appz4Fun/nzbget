What this demonstration establishes

The real caller was reached for every backend, exact output bytes matched, and the intentionally broken route was rejected before timing. Small-read controls are often tight. Several 16 MiB controls have wide intervals, and two normal-read controls resolve a small difference despite identical code. The whole experiment remains inconclusive for production acceptance.

The C++ and Rust single-buffer alternatives are close on normal reads; their shared improvement over the original is not evidence of a Rust-specific advantage. Tiny reads retain the original buffer algorithm. Paired ratios and standalone median times are different statistics and can disagree when runtime varies.

Reproducibility and evidence

Intervals come from nanobench’s paired median log-ratio sign test, adjusted for the three comparisons within each process. They are conditional on the framework’s independence assumptions; they are not combined confidence intervals across all three processes. The ±2% control band is a prospective precision diagnostic, not a production no-regression tolerance. A shared linker router adds harness overhead to all variants; the PoC is not the shipping binary.
